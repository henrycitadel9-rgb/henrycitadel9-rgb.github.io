I presented this research at the **College of Education International Conference 2026** at the University of Ghana. The presentation focused on my study comparing how students interact with a traditional FAQ webpage, a retrieval-based chatbot, and an AI-powered chatbot when accessing the same university information.

I discussed differences across the three interfaces in **ease of use, mental effort, trust, speed, and accuracy**, and what the findings suggest for designing more effective and user-friendly student information systems.
