## Same Facts, Different Interfaces: Evaluating FAQ, Retrieval Chatbot, and AI Chatbot for Student Services

**College of Education International Conference 2026**  
University of Ghana | March 25, 2026

Henry presented this research at the College of Education International Conference 2026 at the University of Ghana. The presentation focused on his study comparing how students interact with a traditional FAQ webpage, a retrieval-based chatbot, and an AI-powered chatbot when accessing the same student-service information. The study examined users' ease of use, mental effort, trust, speed, and accuracy across the three interfaces.
