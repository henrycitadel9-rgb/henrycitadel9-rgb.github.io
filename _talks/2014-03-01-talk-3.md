Henry presented this work at the College of Education International Conference 2026 at the University of Ghana. The presentation examined how different interfaces for accessing the same student-service information, including a traditional FAQ page, a retrieval-based chatbot, and an AI chatbot, influence users' experiences. The study considered factors including ease of use, mental effort, trust, speed, and accuracy.
