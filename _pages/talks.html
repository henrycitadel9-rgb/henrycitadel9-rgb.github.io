Presentation 1 | Oral Presentation

Same Facts, Different Interfaces: Evaluating FAQ, Retrieval Chatbot, and AI Chatbot for Student Services

College of Education International Conference 2026
University of Ghana | March 25, 2026

Henry presented this research at the College of Education International Conference 2026 at the University of Ghana. The study compared how students interacted with three interfaces for accessing the same student-service information: a traditional FAQ webpage, a retrieval-based chatbot, and an AI-powered chatbot. The work examined users' ease of use, mental effort, trust, speed, and accuracy across the three systems.

Presentation 3 | Oral Presentation

Automatic Speech Recognition for Ga: Exploring Speech Recognition for a Low-Resource Language

Research Lab Presentation
University of Ghana

Henry presented his work on automatic speech recognition for Ga, a low-resource Ghanaian language, to senior colleagues in his research lab. The presentation discussed the development of a Ga speech-recognition system using OpenAI's Whisper architecture and the process of fine-tuning the model for Ga speech transcription.
