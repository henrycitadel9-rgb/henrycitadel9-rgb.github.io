I am a recent BA graduate in **Psychology and Information Studies** from the **University of Ghana**, with self-directed training in computing and software engineering. My interdisciplinary background has shaped my interest in understanding how people seek, process, evaluate, and interact with information and technology.

I have research experience spanning **cognition, human interaction with information systems, and applied AI**. Specifically, I investigated how focused attention influences auditory information processing and memory in *[The Effect of Focused Attention on Attended and Unattended Binaural Listening Task](/portfolio/portfolio-3/)*; examined users' interactions with FAQ, retrieval-based chatbot, and AI-chatbot interfaces in *[Same Facts, Different Interfaces: Evaluating FAQ, Retrieval Chatbot, and AI Chatbot for Student Services](/portfolio/portfolio-1/)*; and developed an automatic speech recognition system for Ga, a low-resource Ghanaian language, in *[Building a High-Accuracy Ga ASR System Using the Transformer-Based Whisper Architecture](/portfolio/portfolio-2/)*.

These experiences have strengthened my interest in graduate research at the intersection of **human behavior, information, and computing**.

See more details about my [research and projects](/portfolio/) and [presentations](/talks/).

## Research Interests
